About Us

Trail Quest Productions is run by Lee and Kelly — full-time travelers, riders and outdoor storytellers.

Together for 20 years, we've spent 19 of them deeply involved in outdoor recreation, leading, teaching and exploring.

For the last 11+ years, we've traveled full-time, riding, paddling, hiking and experiencing the same kinds of destinations we create media for.

That experience shapes how we tell outdoor stories — from the perspective of the people actually deciding where to go next.
